RPI (Robert Power Index) National Champions

A historical archive of the teams that finished each season ranked No. 1 in the Robert Power Index.

2025

Indiana

16-0

RPI Rating: 664.53

Notes:

Big Ten Champions Rose Bowl Champions Peach Bowl Champions CFP National Champions First 16-0 season since 1894 What else needs to be said?

2024

Ole Miss

10-3

RPI Rating: 526.40

Notes:

Gator Bowl Champions Took no mercy on Duke after just missing a trip to Atlanta. 2 spots away from getting a shot at the playoffs but their body of work was enough for the Robert Power Index to declare them national champions.

2023

Michigan

15-0

RPI Rating: 539.03

Notes:

Big Ten Champions Rose Bowl Champions CFP National Champions 5th ever 15-0 season in college football history. Michigan rode a dominant defense that allowed just 10.4 points per game and a balanced offense to the national championship.

2022

Georgia

15-0

RPI Rating: 583.46

Notes:

SEC Champions Peach Bowl Champions CFP National Champions Not satisfied after their 14-1 championship season in 2021 Kirby Smart and the Georgia Bulldogs laid waste to the rest of college football in 2022. Culminating in a historic 65-7 victory over TCU in the CFP National Championship Game, Georgia cemented their place as one of the greatest teams in college football history.

2021

Georgia

14-1

RPI Rating: 710.19

Notes:

Orange Bowl Champions CFP National Champions A loss to Alabama in the SEC Title game is all that spoiled one of most dominant season in recent college football history. The Bulldogs rode an elite defense that allowed just 10.2 points per game and paired it with an efficient offense. The Bulldogs ultimately got their revenge on the Crimson Tide by winning the rematch in the CFP title game 33-18.

2020

Clemson

10-2

RPI Rating: 470.04

Notes:

ACC Champions Sugar Bowl/CFP Appearance The asterisk season to end all asterisk seasons. The Tigers loss to Ohio State was not enough to drop them from number 1 in the RPI. In a covidless world it would've been fun to see what Trevor Lawrence and Travis Etienne could've done in their final college season, but alas we'll never know.

2019

Clemson

14-1

RPI Rating: 584.23

Notes:

ACC Champions Fiesta Bowl Champions. Despite losing to LSU in the CFP Final the computer still chose Clemson as the end of season National Champion. Impressed by their dominant 62-17 win over Virginia in the ACC Title game and their clutch 29-23 Fiesta Bowl win over RPI Runner up Ohio State (your eyes don't decive you LSU was indeed 3rd!) I suppose I can almost see where the model was coming from. (key word being almost) But before you jump on me ESPN's FPI had the final rankings as Ohio State 1st Clemson 2nd and LSU again in 3rd so who knows what magic coach O captured to defeat Clemson on that day in 2020.
