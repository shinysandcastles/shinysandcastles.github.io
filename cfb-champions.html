RPI (Robert Power Index) National Champions

The Robert Power Index National Champion is the team ranked No. 1 in the final RPI standings of each season, regardless of its postseason finish. Unlike the CFP, the RPI evaluates a team's complete statistical body of work, meaning the computer's champion may not always be the team that lifted the trophy.

2018

Clemson

15-0

RPI Rating: 633.9

Notes:

ACC Champions
Cotton Bowl Champions
CFP National Champions

Dabo Swinney's most impressive season at Clemson. The first team to go 15-0 since Penn in 1897. 13 of their 15 wins were by 20 or more points. 7 of those were by 30 or more. Dominant from wire to wire. The Tigers removed all doubt about if they were as good as their numbers indicated when they beat 12-0 Notre Dame 30-3 in the Cotton Bowl. Then in a matchup of the consensus number 1 and number 2 teams Clemson gave Nick Saban his worst loss as Head Coach of the Tide. A 44-16 beatdown over an Alabama team some were calling the best ever. 633.9 is the 3rd highest ever end of season rating cementing 2018 Clemson as one of the best teams in College Football's modern era.

2017

Alabama

13-1

RPI Rating: 633.45

Notes:

Sugar Bowl Champions

CFP National Champions

The Final RPI title for Nick Saban was one of his most iconic teams. The Tide were dominate all season long with their lone loss coming to the Auburn Tigers. This team is remembered for having 3 NFL QBs on the depth chart and sending a then record 12 players to the 2018 NFL Draft 5 of which were taken in the first round as well as one of the most iconic plays in CFP history. Freshman QB Tua Tagovailoa making his competitive debut revived a lifeless Alabama offense in the second half and took the Georgia Bulldogs to overtime. All hoped seemed lost when he took a sack on 1st down pushing the Tide out of field goal range. Then on 2nd and 26 he connected with future Heisman winning WR DeVonta Smith on a 41 yard game winning touchdown pass that will live in the hearts of Alabama fans and haunt the dreams of Georgia fans for decades to come.

2007

Kansas

12-1

RPI Rating: 364.23

Notes:

Orange Bowl Champions

Even in a chaotic season like 2007 someone had to be crowned national champions. RPI chose 1 of the 2 one loss teams remaining at the end of the year, but it was an insanely close race. To give you an idea of how close this was the top 6 teams were separated by only 43 points, In 2025 the gap between 1 and 6 was over 230 points! Despite having a significantly weaker schedule than anyone else in the top 10 RPI was impressed enough with their statistical performance to leave them as number 1 to end the season.
